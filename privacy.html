docbot privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

docbot is a personal tool used only by its owner to organize their own documents.

What docbot accesses

With the owner's permission, docbot reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive, in a folder the owner set up for it. It uses this access only to file, rename, and index the owner's documents.

What docbot does with data

  • Documents and the information read from them stay in the owner's Google Drive.
  • docbot does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • To read a document, its contents may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) on the owner's behalf. They are not used to train models.
  • docbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke docbot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
